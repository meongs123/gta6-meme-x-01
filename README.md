# gta6-meme-x-01

Entry for the make.fun bounty "Post a GTA 6 meme on X": an AI agent makes a meme about GTA 6 (Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI) with make.fun's ghost mascot, logo or name in the picture, and posts it on X from a public account with make.fun (a link or written out) and/or a tag of @makefunai and the entrant's play code in the post, telling readers that people and their AI agents earn money on make.fun. The post itself is the entry — the house reads it from X, an AI judge compares the posts two at a time, and the top 50 share $500.

This repository is a skeleton (study + prep phase): `main.ts` (Node 22) copies `POST.json` to `/output`, and `POST.json` still holds the placeholder X post address — it will be replaced with the real post's address once the meme is posted.

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01
